## 🎯 Design Principles Applied

### Typography & Hierarchy
- ✅ Clear H2 headings (text-3xl, not text-4xl everywhere)
- ✅ Natural line lengths (max-w-3xl for reading comfort)
- ✅ Consistent description sizing (text-lg for intros, text-gray-600)
- ✅ Proper weight hierarchy (font-bold for headings, font-semibold for subheads)

### Spacing & Layout
- ✅ py-16 sections instead of py-20 (less excessive whitespace)
- ✅ Purposeful gaps (gap-6 for cards, gap-8 for major sections)
- ✅ Left-aligned content blocks instead of centered
- ✅ Editorial two-column layouts where appropriate

### Color & Visual Design
- ✅ White and gray-50 backgrounds (alternating)
- ✅ Navy text (gray-900) with blue accents
- ✅ Subtle borders (border-gray-200)
- ✅ Icons in blue-100 backgrounds
- ✅ Minimal use of gradients (only hero)
- ✅ Border-left accents for featured content

### Content & Language
- ✅ Specific, natural language
- ✅ No fake statistics
- ✅ No invented testimonials
- ✅ No exaggerated promises
- ✅ Realistic descriptions
- ✅ Practical benefits

### Components & Interaction
- ✅ Consistent card styling
- ✅ Hover states on clickable cards
- ✅ Icon consistency (lucide-react)
- ✅ Badge usage for labels, not decoration
- ✅ Clear CTAs with arrow icons
- ✅ Expandable FAQ details
